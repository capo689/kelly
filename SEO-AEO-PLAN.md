Prepared September 16, 2026

## Objective

Build a two-region search presence in which Kelly Miller becomes a trusted answer and a credible organic result for Central Oregon and the Central Oregon Coast, then earns visibility for the individual communities she serves.

The strategy is not to repeat city names across generic pages. It is to create the clearest local information architecture, publish genuinely useful community answers, connect those answers to current property opportunities, and reinforce the same business entity everywhere Google and answer engines encounter Kelly.

## What the leaders do repeatedly

### Search architecture

- One indexable page for each city or community.
- Separate pathways for property intent such as waterfront, ocean view, acreage, condos, ranches, resort homes, second homes, and land.
- Current listings embedded on the relevant city or property page.
- Hubs that link down to community pages and community pages that link sideways to related places.
- Descriptive internal anchor text rather than repeated “learn more” links.

### Local relevance

- Copy specific to the place, not a city-name swap.
- Practical local topics such as utilities, access, weather exposure, recreation, neighborhood feel, services, and ownership considerations.
- Clear service-area language in navigation, titles, headings, body copy, and business profiles.
- Location pages supported by local guides, market reports, videos, and listing activity.

### Trust and conversion

- Visible agent identity, license, contact details, brokerage context, and service area.
- Reviews and third-party credibility.
- Detailed bios and transaction process pages.
- Simple next steps: property alerts, valuation, consultation, and home search.
- Consistent name, phone, email, and business information across the site and external profiles.

### Freshness and authority

- Live MLS inventory or frequently refreshed listing pages.
- Monthly or quarterly market updates.
- New listing, price reduction, open-house, and sold-property content.
- Useful guides that earn links and give search engines a reason to recrawl.

### Answer-engine readiness

- Headings phrased around real questions.
- Concise answers immediately following the question.
- Detailed supporting context after the short answer.
- Clear entity and place relationships in structured data.
- Self-contained community explanations that can be quoted accurately without inventing a claim.

## AEO publishing standard

Every new page should pass this test:

1. The page answers one primary question in its first 100 words.
2. The H1 is specific and only used once.
3. Each major subheading represents a real follow-up question or decision.
4. The answer is useful without a sales pitch, then offers Kelly as the next step.
5. Claims identify their source and date. Facts that can change are reviewed on a schedule.
6. The author and Kelly’s relevant experience are visible.
7. The page links to its parent hub, related communities, and a logical service page.
8. Structured data describes visible content only.
9. Original media is placed beside the text it supports.
10. The page is indexable, included in the sitemap, internally linked, fast, mobile-friendly, and measurable.

## Measurement

Track results by cluster, not only by total traffic.

- Non-brand impressions and clicks for Central Oregon and Coast queries.
- Top-three, top-ten, and top-twenty keyword counts by community.
- Google Business Profile calls, website clicks, messages, and direction requests.
- Organic contact actions and appointment starts by landing page.
- Indexed pages, crawl errors, canonical status, and Core Web Vitals.
- Mentions and citations in AI-assisted search results using a consistent monthly query set.
- Referring domains and local links earned.
- Review count, review velocity, rating, and response rate.

No ethical SEO plan can promise a number-one ranking. This plan creates the strongest controllable foundation, then compounds authority through current inventory, first-party expertise, reviews, and locally useful content.
